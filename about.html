About us

A small café run by two friends who wanted a place where nobody hurries you out.

Our story

We opened in 2019 with four tables and one oven. Neighbours kept asking for more bread, so we kept baking more.

Today we serve breakfast, lunch and cakes to about 300 people a day, and we still bake everything on site.

What we promise

Fresh ingredients from local farms, no leftover bread sold the next day, and a table for you even when it is busy.
